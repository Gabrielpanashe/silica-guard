SilicaGuard

Screen. Track. Follow Through.

An occupational lung health screening and referral system for Zimbabwe's artisanal and small scale miners, built so that a miner found at risk actually reaches a hospital.

SilicaGuard supports screening and referral. It does not diagnose. Diagnosis is made at a health facility, by clinicians.

The problem

A large workforce that no health system follows up

More than 500,000 people work in artisanal and small scale mining in Zimbabwe, producing approximately 75 percent of the country's gold in 2025.1,2 In a study of 3,821 of these miners, approximately 19 percent had silicosis, and tuberculosis rates in this population have been reported at 39 times the national average.3,4 The country has two dedicated occupational health clinics, at Gweru and Gwanda.5 The most recent screening programme reached 10,668 miners over three years and saw 73 percent of those needing follow up complete it, and then it ended when its funding did in 2024.6

How it works

Two ways in, one set of clinical rules, and a referral that is followed through

Both ways of screening produce the same risk level from the same transparent clinical rules. The measure SilicaGuard is judged on is whether referred miners reach a facility.

01

Screening at the mine site

A village health worker runs a short structured screening on an Android phone at the mine site. It works fully offline and synchronises when a connection is available.

02

Self screening by USSD

A miner who misses the visit can screen themselves over USSD from any handset, with no smartphone and no internet. The service is built; a shortcode has not yet been allocated.

03

Referral, followed through

A miner at higher risk is referred to a named facility with a deadline, reminders and a short referral code. Hospital staff enter the code to confirm attendance, so completion is measured rather than assumed.

Where we are

Status

SilicaGuard won the Cimas Healthathon 3.0 in August 2026. A pilot is in preparation, subject to ethics approval. No miner has been screened with SilicaGuard yet.
